# The Influence Scorecard

From "Get Anyone To Say Yes" by Marisha Lakhiani, Mindvalley.

Score your copy on Cialdini's seven principles of persuasion, see your weakest principle and what to rewrite first, then download the Order of Yes skill: it interviews you about your page, challenges vague answers and rewrites your copy with you.

- `index.html`: the scorecard (single page, no build step)
- `order-of-yes.zip`: the Claude skill, downloaded from the page
- `order-of-yes-chatgpt-skill.zip`: the skill for ChatGPT Skills (upload as-is)
- `order-of-yes-chatgpt.zip`: the ChatGPT prompt pack (custom GPT instructions, knowledge files and a paste-in prompt)
