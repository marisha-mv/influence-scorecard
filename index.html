    Your tool: the Order of Yes skill

    Everything from the session, turned into a tool for Claude or ChatGPT. It reads your page, interviews you, challenges your vague answers, and then rewrites your copy with you.

    How to use it in Claude
    1. 1
      Download the skillSave the Claude skill file to your computer.
    2. 2
      Add it to ClaudeOpen Claude’s settings, find Skills, and upload the file. Skills need code execution turned on, which you’ll find in the same settings.
    3. 3
      Start a new chat and share your pageSay: “Help me fix my sales page,” and paste your page, a link or a screenshot. It asks about your buyer, your story, your proof and your offer, pushes back when you’re vague, then rewrites your page section by section.
    How to use it in ChatGPT
    1. 1
      Download the ChatGPT packIt has four files: instructions.md, interview.md, toolkit.md and full-prompt.md.
    2. 2
      Quickest way, any planStart a new chat, paste everything from full-prompt.md as your first message, then share your page when it asks.
    3. 3
      Best way, to reuse it: make your own GPTIn ChatGPT, go to GPTs and create one. Under Configure, paste instructions.md into Instructions and upload interview.md and toolkit.md as Knowledge. Name it “Fix My Page” and save. Creating GPTs needs a paid ChatGPT plan.
    4. 4
      Say: “Help me fix my sales page”Then paste your page, a link or a screenshot, and answer its questions.
