    Your tool: the Order of Yes skill

    Everything from the session, turned into a guide for Claude or ChatGPT. It reads your website or funnel, helps you nail your audience, asks about anything your pages don’t show, checks every page for the right ingredients, shows you how to do each one better, and rewrites your copy with you.

    How to use it in Claude
    1. 1
      Download the skillSave the Claude skill file to your computer.
    2. 2
      Add it to ClaudeOpen Claude’s settings, find Skills, and upload the file. Skills need code execution turned on, which you’ll find in the same settings.
    3. 3
      Start a new chatSay: “Help me fix my website copy.” It asks for the links to your website or funnel, reads every page, then guides you step by step. Turn on web search in Claude so it can open your links.
    How to use it in ChatGPT
    1. 1
      If your ChatGPT has SkillsSkills are on ChatGPT business and work plans. Download the ChatGPT skill, keep it as a .zip, then in ChatGPT open Skills, choose Create, then Upload a skill, and select the file.
    2. 2
      If it doesn’t: make your own GPTDownload the prompt pack and unzip it. In ChatGPT, go to GPTs and create one. Under Configure, paste everything from instructions.md into Instructions, and upload interview.md, checklist.md, playbook.md and toolkit.md as Knowledge, and turn on Web Search. Name it “Fix My Page” and save. Creating GPTs needs a paid plan.
    3. 3
      On a free planUnzip the prompt pack, start a new chat, and paste everything from full-prompt.md as your first message.
    4. 4
      Then say: “Help me fix my website copy”It asks for the links to your website or funnel, reads every page, and guides you step by step.
